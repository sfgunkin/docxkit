# docxkit — code review, 2026-10-14

A `/code-review max` of everything since the last one: `4deb0f9..HEAD`
(`2473416`), 13 commits, ~730 changed source lines plus tests. It covers
the `[batch] classify` wiring, `tracked.annotate_in_place` and
`comments.annotate_blocks`, the batch-deletion excuse in `baseline` and
`ingest --check`, the bookmark-dropping views (`e461519`), the
`withdraw` rework, the registry TEMP guard and the new revision kinds.

**Process.** Nine finder angles (line-by-line split three ways), and
every finding listed was checked by its own verifier and reproduced,
mostly by scratch scripts against the real code and scratch copies of
real manuscripts. Word itself was not run; where a finding says what
Word does, it is reasoned from or measured on Word-saved files. Nothing
in the repo, the papers or the registry was written.

**Verdict.** Fifteen confirmed findings and eleven smaller ones. Two
lose data silently (1, 2). One breaks every default `revision build`
the moment a paper sets `[batch] classify` (5). Most of the rest share
one shape: the comment path was written for `annotate_in_place` and
the classified `tracked.build` path was wired to `revision build`
without walking the hardening across — credit, the template, the
`generic=None` promise and the scaffold-less case each hold on one of
the two paths and not the other.
